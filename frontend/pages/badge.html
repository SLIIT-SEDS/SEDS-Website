Discover The SEDS SLIIT Badge Series

At SEDS SLIIT, we believe in recognizing and rewarding the hard work, dedication, and passion of our members. The SEDS Badge Series is more than just a collection of digital badges—it’s a year-long ranking program that highlights our members' achievements in astronomy and space exploration activities.

The purpose of this badge series is to,
  • Identify and celebrate the silent heroes who make a difference behind the scenes.
  • Appraise the commitment of students who actively contribute to meetings, events, and tasks
  • Award valuable e-badges to deserving members to inspire continued excellence.
This program is designed to track performance throughout the year, based on three main criteria,
  1. Attendance at Meetings – Demonstrating consistency and involvement.
  2. Event Contributions – Actively participating in society events.
  3. Tasks Completed – Completing assigned responsibilities effectively.

At the end of the year, the scores from these areas are averaged to determine a final performance score, which is used to award badges.

But it doesn’t end there! At the conclusion of our time in SEDS SLIIT, members will receive a certificate imprinted with all the badges they earned during their time in the society. This certificate will be a lasting recognition of their efforts, adding significant value to their achievements. Looking ahead, 2025 will bring two new badge series, expanding the opportunities for members to earn and showcase their talents.
